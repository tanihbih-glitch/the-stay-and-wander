# Barcelona vs Lisbon 2026 — source notes

Retrieved 2026-10-07 for `/blog/barcelona-vs-lisbon-2026`.

## Barcelona

- Radical Storage, “Is Barcelona Expensive? 2026 Cost Guide”: hostel dorm €22–40; budget hotel/guesthouse €55–95; mid-range 3-star €95–190; luxury 4–5-star €190–550+; the guide says summer (June–August) is the highest price level and hotel rates often double. It also reports Barcelona hotel averages around €110–130 in 2026, but the comparison uses the tier bands rather than presenting that as a citywide live average.
- Rick Steves, “When to Go to Barcelona”: late spring and early fall offer lighter crowds; July and August bring hot, humid weather and the biggest crowds, and some shops/restaurants close in August.

## Lisbon

- LisbonLisboaPortugal.com, “The cost of hotels in Lisbon for 2026”: standard double room averages including breakfast of €153 for 3-star, €220 for 4-star, and €365 for 5-star. The guide says these are year-round averages, roughly shoulder-season context, and that peak-month prices can rise about 50%; June–September is premium, with July and August at peak pricing and tighter availability.
- The Stay & Wander, “Where to Stay in Lisbon 2026”: published internal starting example from $45/night and neighborhood-fit guidance for Alfama, Chiado/Baixa, Bairro Alto/Príncipe Real, Belém, and Avenida/Estrela.
- Rick Steves, “When to Go to Portugal”: summer months are the most crowded and expensive in coastal Portugal, especially July and August; spring and fall offer a better weather/crowd balance.

## Boundary note

These figures are dated planning benchmarks, not live quotes or a property ranking. The page directs travelers to the live Trip.com handoff to verify dates, occupancy, taxes, room type, availability, and cancellation terms.
