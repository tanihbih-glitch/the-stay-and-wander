## Phuket research sources (external, used as planning context, not live quotes)
- https://www.phuket-escape.com/guides/phuket-travel-budget/
  - Budget accommodation: 300–700 THB/night (~$9–$21); mid-range accommodation: 1,500–3,500 THB/night (~$46–$107); luxury accommodation: 5,000–25,000+ THB/night (~$153–$767+); private pool villas from ~8,000 THB/night for a two-bedroom to 50,000+ THB at top resorts.
  - Budget food 200–400 THB/day; mid-range food 600–1,200 THB/day; luxury food 1,500–4,000 THB/day.
  - Budget transport 100–200 THB/day; mid-range 300–500 THB/day. Songthaew rides 30–50 THB; typical 15-minute Grab 80–150 THB; scooter 400–600 THB/day.
  - Phuket high season November–April; low season May–October; source says low-season hotel rates can drop 30–50%, with weather/sea-condition trade-offs.
- https://www.expedia.com/Phuket-Hotels.d6046393.Travel-Guide-Hotels
  - Live search page displayed `Find hotels in Phuket from $10` for its current default search, and lists 2,236 3-star, 875 4-star, and 226 5-star properties; rates depend on dates, occupancy, taxes, and availability.
  - Use only as evidence that live inventory/rates are date-dependent; do not present $10 as a stable benchmark.
- https://northabroad.com/where-to-stay-phuket-best-hotels/
  - Area-fit guidance: Karon and Kata are family-friendly / swim-friendly first-trip bases; Patong is the nightlife-heavy choice; Surin/Kamala are calmer; Bang Tao is known for luxury resorts and lagoon developments; Phuket Old Town offers more affordable accommodation and cultural/boat-trip access; Mai Khao is quiet but sea can be rough.
