## Comparative safeguards

The matrix uses **documented-fit signals**, not scores of hotel quality, value, current availability, or sustainability performance. A **3/3** signal means a first-party UAE property source documents a strong relevant feature; **2/3** means a brand or corporate source supports the general format or framework but not a property-level comparative result; **1/3** means the available information is insufficient for local reliance. The selector uses only source-verified UAE properties, reports why a result is included, applies a stable deterministic tie-break, and always tells readers to confirm live availability, inclusions, loyalty eligibility, and cancellation terms.

Hilton remains in the guide’s comparison framework because Homewood Suites and Home2 Suites are extended-stay formats, but the selector must not imply that either is currently available in the UAE without a verified UAE property source. Marriott Executive Apartments is similarly separated from the selector while its official page reports reservations as unavailable.
