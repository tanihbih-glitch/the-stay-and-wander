## Intent exclusions

Do not build keyword briefs or new destination content around long, sentence-length prompts—regardless of language—about hotel-group sustainability, loyalty programs, or accessibility. These queries may surface incidentally in Search Console but are not treated as prospect-demand signals. This does not remove relevant factual guidance already present in a guide.

## UAE guide audit outcome

The UAE article is listed on the Blog index and links back to the Dubai/Abu Dhabi guide. No backlinks were fabricated; external link acquisition remains a separate outreach decision.
