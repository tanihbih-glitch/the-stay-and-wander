# Dubai–Abu Dhabi and Tokyo–Osaka source notes (2026-10-07)

## Dubai / Abu Dhabi local-knowledge boundaries
- Official Dubai tourism: https://www.visitdubai.com/en/
  - Dubai tourism site describes Dubai as a metropolis with distinct neighbourhood stories, traditional arts/crafts and cultural gems, modern malls and traditional souks, record-breaking landmarks, nightlife/events, and food/drink experiences.
  - Use for supported copy: Dubai works as a high-contrast, attraction-dense city base where skyline landmarks, shopping, dining, nightlife/events, and neighbourhood choice shape the trip.
- Official Abu Dhabi tourism: https://visitabudhabi.ae/en
  - Official site foregrounds culture, Saadiyat Cultural District, Sheikh Zayed Grand Mosque, Qasr Al Watan, family attractions, relaxation, beaches, itineraries, and a city map/transport planning.
  - Use for supported copy: Abu Dhabi is a culture-and-family-oriented capital with major museums/heritage landmarks, beaches/relaxation, family attractions, and a less attraction-dense rhythm than Dubai. Avoid unsupported claims about exact crowd levels or universal quietness.

## Tokyo / Osaka local-knowledge boundaries
- Official Tokyo tourism first-timer guide: https://www.gotokyo.org/en/story/walks-and-tours/for-the-first-timer/index.html
  - Tokyo is a vast metropolis of districts with distinct character; official first-timer coverage includes fish markets, Ginza, and historic Asakusa, and advises planning for walking and transit transfers.
  - Use for supported copy: Tokyo suits first-time visitors who want breadth, district variety, major rail flexibility, shopping, and layered urban/cultural exploration.
- Japan National Tourism Organization Osaka guide: https://www.japan.travel/en/destinations/kansai/osaka/
  - JNTO describes Osaka as relaxed, food/fun/nightlife-oriented, with history and culture; highlights Dotonbori/Minami, Osaka Castle, Tenma/Ura Namba, and Osaka as a base for Kyoto, Nara, Kobe and Wakayama. It states Tokyo–Osaka Shinkansen takes about 2.5 hours.
  - Use for supported copy: Osaka is a relaxed, food-led and nightlife-friendly base with strong Kansai day-trip utility and a distinct personality from Tokyo.
- Official Osaka tourism: https://osaka-info.jp/en/
